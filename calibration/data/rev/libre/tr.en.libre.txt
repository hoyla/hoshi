Port authorities paid two times to the consulting firm for the same report.

Westmere Port Authorities paid 186,000 pounds to a consulting firm for a mud cleaning work in March 2019, and then paid to the same company, after ten four months, for a second study 172,500 £.500. Two reports were almost identical. When we compare the reports line, we found that 41 of the 48 pages are the same, even a typing error on the 12th page is also common.

There was no tender for both payments. According to the specific rules of the authorities, three written offers are required for each contract over 50.000 pounds. Margaret Oduya, CEO of the authorities, said that this rules are not violated, because the study is a "emergency and requires expertise."

However, this situation was transferred differently to the board. In the holding of a meeting held on May 9, 2020, it was noted that the second study was defined as a new evaluation by a new team. But the presence of such a new team was not concerned. Both report also signed two engineers and one of them was separated from the company before the second report was delivered.

Tidewell Associates, a consultant firm, registered in Portbury. The only manager of the company is Daniel Reyes and served in the financial committee of port authorities up to 2017. Mr Reyes did not respond to five email and two mektuba. When we arrive with the phone, we ended the conversation by saying there is nothing to add.

A person who previously worked as a financial officer in port authorities and did not want to explain his name, said that he expressed his concerns in writing three times. "Bana said that it was approved at the top level and the bill should be processed." No time a description has been made. After four months, the position was removed from work, but the authorities indicate that these two events are not associated.

The mud cleaning work was still not done. According to a research conducted for the insurance companies of the port in January, the mud in the main channel is 1.8 meters deeper than the safe limit. This year the two freight ships were rewinded. The third one was installed in the bunker in February and had to wait for ten hours to wait for the rise of the tide.

Local businesses say they paid their price. The fish market has decreased about three sales since 2021. A ferry bakım cancels the summer time between the islands and the owner of the boat care workshop said that it had to remove six of the tens of employees.

Port authorities receive 2,4 million public funds annually. Since 2020, it has not been published audited financial tables. Regional auditor confirmed that an investigation was launched in the last fall, but he did not specify when the investigation will result or whether two payments are part of the investigation.

In a written statement, the port authorities stated that "we took their place seriously" and "he was authorized in accordance with all payments." It didn’t respond to our questions as the report was approved by who the second payment is the same, or Mr Reyes did not declare the conflict of interest. Also refused to release invoices.

We asked the Ministry of Transport to be informed of these payments before they renew the grant of the authorities. The ministry spokesperson said that the ministry did not comment on individual cases. Local folksandkili Hannah Whitlock calls to stack the board.
