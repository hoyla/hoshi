Harbour Authority paid the advisor twice for the same report.

Westmer Harbour Authority paid 186,000 pounds to a consultancy for a drainage study in March 2019, and then paid 172,500 pounds to the same firm after fourteen months. Both reports are almost the same. We lined their comparison line rate and found that 41 pages out of 48 pages are alike, including a typing error on page 12.

The tender process for both payments has not been followed. According to its rules of authorization, three written quotes are required for any contract worth more than 50,000 pounds. Its Chief Executive Officer, Margaret Oduya, told us that the rules did not violate because this work was “neutral and specific”.

The Board was not told. The second study that recorded in minutes of a meeting that took place on 9th May 2020 was “a new assessment made by a new team”. No new team existed. The same two engineers signed on both reports, and one of them left the firm before the release of the second report.

This consultancy, “Tidwell Associates”, is registered at a residential address at Portbury. Its sole director, Daniel Reyes, was a member of the Finance Committee of the Harbour Authority by 2017. He did not respond to five emails and two letters. When we contacted them on a telephone, he said they have nothing to say and they finished the conversation.

A former finance officer of the authority who requested not to uncover his name, said he expressed concerns three times in writing. “I was told that it has been approved at the top level and I should process the invoice,” he said. They have never been told for any reason. Four months later their job was finished, however the authority says that both events are unrelated.

The work of drainage has not started yet. According to a survey conducted for the insurers of the port in January, the silt in the main channel is now 1.8 meters deeper than the safe range. Two cargo vessels have been returned this year. A third ship trapped in sand in February and had to stop him for eleven hours to wait for the tide.

Local businesses say they’re paying its price. The fish market has lost nearly one-fiveth part business since 2021. A yacht operator canceled his summer service for the islands, and the owner of the boatyard told us that he has fired six of his fourteen workers.

Authorization gets 2.4 million pounds per year as public wealth. It has not published accounts audited from 2020. The Regional Auditor confirmed that an investigation was initiated in the past autumn, but they did not tell when it will report, or whether these are part of two payment investigations.

In a written statement, the authority said that it “takes its responsibilities seriously” and “all payments were appropriately authorized”. He did not answer our questions why reports are similar, who approved the second payment, or did Shri Reyes declare a conflict of interest. He also refused to issue invoices.

We have asked the Ministry of Transport whether they knew about these payments before grant renewal. A spokesperson said that the ministry does not comment on personal matters. Local MP, Hannah Whitlock, has sought resignation of the Board.
